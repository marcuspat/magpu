Show notes

Cliff says:

The show opened up with Mama Mello and closed with Olospo. I think this show featured our spectacular light system for extra visual enjoyment. I think it completely went out at one point during Olospo’s set, leaving the band in complete darkness right at a key moment in one of their songs. But they played on.

This recording is a “retroactive matrix” of the audience and soundboard recordings.
