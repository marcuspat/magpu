Recreational Music

Recreational Music is Magpu’s first comprehensive written-song-oriented studio album. It complements both the purely improvisational Project Pu, and the many freely available live “audience” recordings. It presents Magpu’s compositional abilities, with the usual improvisational embellishments, in studio-quality sound.

For information about ordering a copy, visit the contact page.
