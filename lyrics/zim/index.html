Poetry by Chris Zimmerly

From time to time, Chris Zimmerly collaborates with Magpu in live improvisation, lending his imaginative words and distinctive voice. Here, you'll find Chris's transcriptions of the poems that he crafted over Magpu's accompanyment on various occasions.
