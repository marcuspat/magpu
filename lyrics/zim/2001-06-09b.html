Oasis Drum
Red Blood Club patio - Dallas, TX
9 June 2001
© Stephen Christian Zimmerly and Cliff McCarthy, 2001

Zim:

"Across the wide tracks of the desert
We, the camel caravan, move through
Hot radiating moments of sun billows up
Causing ripples waves move through our skin HOT
Hot camel caravan, somewhere out there is the oasis
Somewhere out there is sanctuary, somewhere�
Find what you are looking for out in hot desert sands
Burns the innocent fingers and toes
Burns those who do not know
In the oasis we raise great tents,
Spices from far far away, these moments drifting
Fragrances that pass by your nose, lingers on your tongue
It is the movement of flesh, the movement of silk,
The movement of Persia gathers us all into the oasis
The women dance, the singers sing, the musicians bring all
Forward that which has never been seen
We pack up the hookah full with hashish
Kind bud from far reaches of the world
We pull, everyone on octopus tentacles in the mouth
Drawing deep inside the velvet curtains of the tent
The women all dance around the scents the sights the sounds
As we draw in ourselves, yes, higher and higher we go"

Cliff:

"I've been riding for days
I didn't know where I was anymore,
I thought maybe this was the place
I didn't know anymore"

Zim:

"All are welcome in the oasis, all is good in the oasis,
In this sanctuary like the planet earth in the universal void
We come together--you better be kind, you better be fine
You better spread some love around or
We'll put you in the ground like seeds
Waiting to grow when water comes again, we live in a desert"

Cliff:

"I can smell the city from miles away-spices, the women
...I am a sanctuary, but I had to keep going,
Keep going...a few more miles"

Zim:

"Some can't stay in the oasis, there is something
Inside them twirls them rapidly like a yo-yo
Into the dark night-the never night, when the string
Breaks they disappear, these people are gone
We wish them well as they travel on with the kiss of grace,
The kiss of books, of thoughts, everything,
We send them further out into the void
To maybe someday come back telling us
What is beyond our simple moment here."
