Ride the Tide

words and music by Brian Cox

river bottom
crystal currents
clinging creatures
a way of life

tired of clinging
one creature flies:
"trust the current,
it knows the way..."

ride the tide wherever it goes
the spirit knows where the current flows

floating downstream back to my home
gonna ride the current to the noodle dome

"argue for your,
limitations...
sure enough,
they are yours..."

trust in the one and ride the tide
surrender to the moment and become alive
