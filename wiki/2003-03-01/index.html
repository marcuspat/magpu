2003-03-01 - Denton, TX

On 1 March 2003, Magpu's Brian Cox, Kurt Kistler, and Cliff McCarthy got together with DentonMark's neighbour Matt and jammed in Brian's living room. The best moments have been extracted and are presented here for your musical enjoyment.

Line-up:

  • Brian Cox: guitar
  • Kurt Kistler: keyboards, loops
  • Cliff McCarthy: bass
  • Matt of the Unknown Last Name: guitar
