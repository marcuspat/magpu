Magpu made music from 1998 to 2003, primarily around the Dallas - Fort Worth area. We still like the music we made, and hope you enjoy it too.

magpu.com went through three very different versions while the band was together (and after). This is a recreation of the whole site, built from the Internet Archive — pick a version above and start browsing. New to Magpu? Hear the very first show, from Club Dada in 1998.
