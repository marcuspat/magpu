Escher's Elevator is a new collaboration between Kurt Kistler and Cliff McCarthy. Drawing on our years of improvisational interaction as members of Magpu, we aim to create improvised music that visits non-traditional sonic realms, while also incorporating more familiar elements. This music tends to be fluid and gradual; themes and arrangements are created or discovered on the spot and evolved by the demands of the moment. We gladly share our music with anyone who takes the time to listen, and we thank you for visiting us here on the web!
