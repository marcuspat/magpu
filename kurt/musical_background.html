MY MUSICAL BACKGROUND

I was born in Houston, Texas on October 6th, 1968. So, technically, I am a child of the sixties. My whole life, I have gravitated towards artistic endeavors. Acting, animation, filmmaking, drawing, painting, digital art, drumming, hand percussion, piano and synthesizers, and recording.

I am a HUGE Star Wars fan and can honestly say that one of the first melodies I ever picked out by ear was the Star Wars theme! That movie did something special in my 9 year-old mind: It made me truly aware of how all art can be channelled into one medium and produce fantastic, imaginative results. I started playing the piano when I was 10 years old in 1978 and continued taking lessons through high school - a total of eight years. (I play almost entirely by ear because it takes too long to read the notes on a staff!)

From 6th-9th grade, I played the Trombone. In 8th grade, I remember vividly getting ten records for a penny from Columbia House and getting turned on to The Police Synchronicity, Rush Signals, and Genesis Genesis. My Freshman year in Dulles High School (1983), I was involved in the Marching, Concert and Jazz bands along with acting in Theater. Not counting piano recitals, the first official concert I ever played was at my high school graduation in 1987 from Subiaco Academy, a Catholic College-Preperatory Boarding School run by Benedictine Monks, as some part of a talent show with a band that was thrown together by three other friends of mine. My keyboard at the time was a Yamaha PSR-30 - with 1/2 size keys and a killer samba rhythm loop (in retrospect - it sucked, but you have to start somewhere!). If I'm correct, we played "Separate Ways" by Journey and the "Love Theme-Instrumental" from Top Gun.

At this point, I had just started to begin my journey through all kinds and various forms of music - Pink Floyd, Genesis, Peter Gabriel, Art of Noise, The Beatles, Tangerine Dream, Jean-Michel Jarre, and Vangelis.....to name a few.

In 1987, I attended college at North Texas State University in Denton. While there, I majored in Radio/TV/Film while minoring in Art. To say the least, I could show more from my Art classes than the film classes after a couple of years. Having already worked on my own student films utilizing stop-action animation, lighting, and special effects, I felt that this was a step backwards - even if they had to start everyone out on the same page. I also hated being a number in a sea of students.

My first Psychedelic experience happened on Friday, September 22, 1988 - the doors of perception were flung wide-open - the rewiring under the floorboards of my brain had begun and thus changed my outlook and perceptions about the Universe and my place in it...forever.

I got turned on to King Crimson during the Fall of 1988 at UNT by a hippie down the hall in my dorm. He let me borrow In The Court of The Crimson King and In The Wake of Poseidon (I didn't fully "get it" until I re-discovered them in 1994) along with Pink Floyd's "Atom Heart Mother".

While learning how to multi-track record and use synthesizers outside of class, I had the opportunity to record and collaborate with sax players, other keyboardists, and percussionists for some of my early solo recordings. Being able to jam with anyone down the hall in my dorm was a very educational experience. In 1989, I dropped out of UNTand enrolled in The Art Institute of Dallas in their Visual Communications program and re-enrolled again in 1995 in the Computer Animation/Multimedia Program.

During the Spring and Summer of 1989, I was immersed in Genesis' The Lamb Lies Down on Broadway album and a lot of classic rock. While living in a "practically condemned" apartment during the summer of 1989, affectionately called: The Pig's Ass, I played a drumkit without cymbals and two other friends on Guitar and Vocals with a dubbed-in synth bass for as long as we could get away with it before The Man showed up.

In 1990, I re-discovered the music of Rush,Yes, Jimi Hendrix, Brand X and early Genesis and started diving into the back catalogs of their albums - absorbing all like a sponge.

By 1991, I began soaking up Hawkwind, Emerson, Lake & Palmer, Butthole Surfers, The Moody Blues and Jethro Tull. I also was involved in my first real Rock band called: Touch. We just rehearsed alot at the Guitarist's house, rented a rehearsal studio to record and play for a couple of times at and never gigged once.

In the Spring of 1992, I got my first honest-to-goodness synthesizer (Korg M1) with an 8-track sequencer built-in as a graduation present from my Dad. And thus began the learning curve of how to record digitally, sequence, and write layered music. I also started jamming with some high school friends from Subiaco in a band called: Ick. We played a show in The Rathskeller (an underground bar at Univ. of Dallas) and at Joe's Garage in Fort Worth.

In 1993-94, I joined a band of all Art Institute students called Fate Rains that was like Psychedelic Spaghetti Western music.

By 1994-95, I was immersed in the music of Ozric Tentacles, Frank Zappa, King Crimson, Phish, Primus Ween and the Grateful Dead.

Critters Buggin, Hovercraft, Moe and Medeski, Martin & Wood expanded my mind and musical horizons in 1996.

By 1997-98, I was turned on to The Mahavishnu Orchestra and Miles Davis and started to go out and experience live bands such as Phish, King Crimson (Projeckt 3), The Disco Biscuits, Moe, Galactic, String Cheese Incident, Widespread Panic, Medeski, Martin & Wood.
