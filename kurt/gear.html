MY GEAR

My "RIG" consists of a Roland VK-7 Organ, a Korg Prophecy solo synthesizer, a Korg MS2000 rack unit synth, and a Roland JV35 keyboard.

My effects consist of a LINE 6 delay modeler, Boss Super Phase-Shifter pedal, Ibanez Delay/Echo pedal, Alesis Nanoverb, Crybaby Wah pedal, and a Real-Tube overdrive pedal.

I also have a variety of percussion instruments including a 14" Djimbe, tablas, Dumbek, wind gong, small gong, small ankle bells, finger cymbals, sleighbells, nectarine and apple shakers, Gana tambourine, polished pecan shells, kono nut shells, tiny shells, tiny goat hooves, and a rain stick.
