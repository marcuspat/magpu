THE BEGINNING OF THE 'PU'

I met Brian quite unexpectedly at a Waltermitty show at Club Dada one Saturday afternoon Summer of 1997. Actually, I met Stan (the taper) and he told me that Brian was looking to jam with some other musicians and I ought to go talk to him. Brian asked me who some of my influences were and I just so happened to unknowingly compile a list earlier that week of all of my musical influences. So I handed him the piece of paper that was in my wallet...and that answered all of the questions. Within a couple of weeks, we started jamming together with Kyle (the GIANT bass player) and a drummer at a Crackhouse motel/Rehearsal space complete with blue shag carpet. It was okay, but slow and kind of directionless. One day, about four months and twenty days later, Brian called me and told me about Terry's ad in The Observer: "PLANO DRUMMER SEEKS ADVANCED MUSICIANS FOR MAHAVISHNU / ZAPPA / KING CRIMSON TYPE RECREATIONAL MUSIC MAKING." It was a musical match made in heaven.

And thus, Magpu was born.
